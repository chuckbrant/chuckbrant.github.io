<p>Today started with a boat ride to Murano to watch glassblowers at work, then continues into Venice proper for a walking tour through the alleyways and over the bridges to St. Mark's Square - the pink marble Doge's Palace, the Bridge of Sighs, and the golden mosaics of the Basilica di San Marco. The rest of the day is free to wander on our own and we took a gondola ride.</p>
