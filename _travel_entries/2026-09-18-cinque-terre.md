<p>We spend the day at Cinque Terre - the five cliffside villages of the Ligurian coast that make up a UNESCO World Heritage national park. Its listing is technically a "cultural landscape," which is UNESCO's way of saying the villages themselves aren't the whole point - it's the thousand-plus years of terraced vineyards carved into near-vertical cliffs that matters, an entire agricultural system engineered by hand onto terrain that has no business supporting farmland at all. It's an active day if we go: uneven ground, stairs, real slopes, moving between towns by train.</p>
