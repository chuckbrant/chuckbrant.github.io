<p>This morning we take a scenic ferry across the lake to Bellagio, which has earned its nickname "The Pearl of Lake Como", it sits on the promontory where the lake splits into its two southern branches, with earth-toned buildings climbing up from the water and narrow lanes full of craft shops and cafes to get lost in.</p>

<p></p>

<p>In Bellagio we had gelato for lunch.</p>

<p></p>

<p>We took a quick trip to Switzerland, a bonus we were not expecting, and had a cold drink by the lake.</p>
