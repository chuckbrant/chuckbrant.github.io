<p>Florence, the city most people mean when they say "the Renaissance." The word itself just means "rebirth" - the idea, starting here in the 1300s and 1400s, that Europe could look past a thousand years of medieval religious art and rediscover the humanism, proportion, and technical ambition of classical Greece and Rome. </p>

<p></p>

<p>It wasn't only art: it was new ways of thinking about the individual, science, and the human body, spread first through Florentine workshops and eventually across all of Europe by way of the printing press.</p>
