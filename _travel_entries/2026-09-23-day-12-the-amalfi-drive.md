<p>The Amalfi Coast road is genuinely one of the most spectacular drives anywhere - narrow curves and hairpin bends threading past whitewashed villas, lemon groves terraced into the cliffs, and turquoise water the whole way</p>

<p></p>

<p>The center of Amalfi town, the Duomo di Sant'Andrea sits at the top of a long staircase rising straight off the main piazza, its striped facade and Arab-Norman bell tower reflecting the town's old trading links across the Mediterranean, not just to the rest of Italy.</p>
