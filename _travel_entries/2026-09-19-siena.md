<p>We had a full day excursion to Siena.</p>

<p></p>

<p>Siena's heart is the shell-shaped Piazza del Campo, built directly over the old Roman forum and still, by most accounts, one of the most beautiful public squares in Italy. </p>

<p></p>

<p>Siena was Florence's great rival for most of the medieval period, and it shows in the art: Sienese Gothic painting, led by artists like Duccio di Buoninsegna, developed its own distinct style of gold-ground, elegantly linear religious painting that stands apart from what was happening in Florence at the same time - a reminder that "Renaissance Italy" was never one single artistic movement so much as a set of fiercely competing city-states.</p>
